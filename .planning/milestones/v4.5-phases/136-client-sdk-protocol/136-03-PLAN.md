<objective>
Fix the `GameConnection` lifecycle races: (SDK-01) add an `opened` promise mirroring the dev-host-client house pattern, and make `action()` await the open (bounded by `connectionTimeout`) then send — rejecting loudly on timeout/failure instead of silently resolving `{success:false}`; (SDK-02) replace the `config.autoReconnect` mutation with a private `#userDisconnected` flag cleared by `connect()`, guard `scheduleReconnect()` on it, and honor the `connectImmediately` config field so the connection can be constructed without dialing.

Purpose: this is the root-cause plan for the two connection footguns — silent action drops (a Repudiation risk: the UI appears to accept an action that never reached the server) and the invisible disconnect/connect asymmetry. Mirror dev-host-client's `opened` promise construction (do NOT import or extend that class — its header forbids sharing); adapt its throw-on-not-open guard to `action()`'s await-then-send requirement per the locked decision.
Output: an awaitable, fail-loud GameConnection consumed by Plans 04 (MeepleClient.connect) and 05 (useGame).
</objective>
