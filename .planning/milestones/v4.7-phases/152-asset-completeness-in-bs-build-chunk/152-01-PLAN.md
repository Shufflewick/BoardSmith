<objective>
Ship a reusable `AssetImage.vue` SFC that every generated game inherits from Chunk 0, so a
missing/unresolved card or piece asset degrades cleanly to a game-semantic drawn fallback instead
of rendering a broken `<img>`. This is the structural half of ASSET-01: broken images become
impossible on the custom-UI path because `AssetImage.vue` is the only sanctioned way to render
asset art.

Purpose: Close DEF-A's class structurally at the scaffold layer, never as a per-game hand-patch.
Output: `generateAssetImageVue()` added to `project-scaffold.ts`'s `generateScaffoldFiles()` array,
emitting `src/ui/components/AssetImage.vue` into every `npx boardsmith init` project, plus unit
coverage in `project-scaffold.test.ts`.
</objective>
