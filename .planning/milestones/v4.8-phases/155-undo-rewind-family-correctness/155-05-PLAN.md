<objective>
Add the client-side defense-in-depth half of UNDO-04 (CONTEXT D-07, final bullet): a client that
reconnects into a rewound session must not carry a stale animation watermark.

Purpose: the server-side fix (Plan 04) makes the sequence monotonic for a client that stayed
connected. A client that joins or reconnects mid-rewind, or that was showing a session before it was
rewound, can still hold a `lastQueuedId` above the ids it is now being sent. This is belt-and-braces
for the primary fix, not a substitute for it.

Output: an always-published `state.actionCount`, watched by `useAnimationEvents` as a rewind signal.
</objective>

<decisions>
- The signal is a **new, always-published `state.actionCount`** (raw
  `runner.actionHistory.length`), per RESEARCH.md §C. It is a count, not content — no visibility or
  hidden-information concern, so it needs no seat gating. Reusing `turnStartActionIndex` was
  evaluated and rejected: it is only sent to the acting player.
- The animation sequence itself cannot be its own rewind detector, because Plan 04 makes it
  monotonic by design.
- **Detection rule:** a DECREASE in `actionCount` between two observations means a rewind happened.
  Reset `lastQueuedId` and `lastProcessedId` to 0 BEFORE processing the incoming `animationEvents`
  batch. An increase or no change changes nothing.
</decisions>
