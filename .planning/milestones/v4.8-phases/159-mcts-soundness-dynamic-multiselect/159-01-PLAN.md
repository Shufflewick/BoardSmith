  the single source of truth called by BOTH enumeration (this plan) and `buildPickMetadata` (Plan 02).

  silent degradation. A required selection that genuinely can't resolve surfaces the throw.
- Any error message the helper adds is actionable and contains NO file paths, line numbers, or stack
  traces (project hard rule).
