<objective>
SKILLAUTO-02..05 (B.2–B.5): codify question discipline, batching, run-while-away, and auto-advance.
- B.2 (ask discipline): ask ONLY when the answer is genuinely undetermined by rules + prior answers AND the choice is load-bearing AND no reasonable default exists; otherwise proceed and RECORD THE ASSUMPTION. Never re-ask an already-granted approval; never route a human playtest for a UI-less chunk (reinforces Plan A).
- B.3 (batching): accumulate open questions into a QUEUE, keep building everything not blocked, and surface the batch at the next human gate/milestone (the GSD-autonomous model).
- B.4/B.5 (run-while-away + auto-advance): keep making progress on reasonable defaults and auto-advance into the next chunk (and the next logical step, e.g. generate-AI → final-acceptance) without the human re-invoking. The printed resume command is retained ONLY as a cold-resume/crash fallback — NOT as a stop signal.

Purpose: Turn the pipeline into a genuinely autonomous run bounded only by Plan A's milestone gates + rules adjudication + context/stuck-state, instead of stalling on avoidable questions or a per-close handoff.
Output: ask-criteria triple-gate + record-assumption in ask.md/build.md, batched-queue + auto-advance prose in state-machine.md, removal of the residual print-and-handoff stop at close.md + build-chunk.md, and fail-pre/pass-post drift tests.
</objective>
