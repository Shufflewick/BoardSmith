<decisions>
## Design decision (pit of success)

CONTEXT D-07 requires distinguishing the two `loadSerializedState` callers. RESEARCH.md §C offers
two shapes: an option parameter, or re-stamping at the four undo/rewind call sites. **Chosen: the
option parameter, with `fromCheckpoint` deriving the floor itself.**

Why: `fromCheckpoint` already holds both the historical checkpoint AND the enclosing live snapshot,
so it can compute `animationSeqFloor = snapshot.state.animationEventSeq` with **no change at any of
the four undo/rewind call sites**. That makes it impossible for a future undo executor to forget the
floor — the correct behavior comes for free from using the sanctioned restore primitive. The
alternative (re-stamp at each call site) duplicates the logic four ways and is exactly the drift
hazard the shared-guard principle exists to prevent.

`GameSession.restore` and `mcts-bot` go through `fromSnapshot` WITHOUT the option and keep today's
behavior (adopting the persisted seq is correct when the process is starting fresh — there is no
live counter to protect).
</decisions>
