<objective>
Close DEVHOST-03 (D15): the dev host orphans its own first seat when a client disconnects mid-`startGame`
await — the seat is reserved to a vanished human, not AI-covered, and the flow loop stalls with nobody
able to act.

Purpose: `startGame` (`multiplayer-host.ts:507-587`) sets `starting=true` (`:508`), captures
`humanSeats` (`:510-512`), `await session.start()` (`:566`), then commits `phase='playing'` and runs
`runAITurns()` (`:580`). If client A disconnects during the `await`, `disconnect` (`:218-233`) only
flips `connected=false` and keeps the seat human. `startGame` resumes with seat 1 = a disconnected
human that `playerIsAI[0]=false` won't drive → the loop stalls, and the reinit `send` to A is dropped
(socket closed). This is DISTINCT from the fixed DEF-C stale-close guard (connection-handler.ts:59-68,
reload-beats-close), which does not reconcile a disconnect that lands inside the start-await.

Output: after `await session.start()`, reconcile human/AI seats against `this.connected` so a
seat whose client vanished during the await is AI-covered (loop proceeds) yet stays reclaimable on
reconnect (not permanently converted to AI), proven by a deterministic interleave regression.
</objective>

State to reconcile against:
- `this.connected: Set<string>` (`:133`) — `disconnect` deletes from it (`:219`); `hello` adds

  present; `rebuildAiSeats()` (`:496-503`) rebuilds from `info.clientId && info.connected`.

<decisions>
## Locked decisions implemented here (CONTEXT.md D15)

- **Reconcile AFTER `await session.start()`**, in the window between `starting=true` (`:508`) and the
  phase commit / reinit (`:574-586`): a seat whose client is no longer in `this.connected` becomes
  AI-covered so `runAITurns` drives it and the loop cannot stall.
- **Reclaimable, not permanent.** The reconciliation only ensures the loop has a driver (bot) WHILE the
  seat is disconnected. Keep the seat reserved to its `clientId` (do NOT release it) so `hello` on
  reconnect reclaims it and the bot yields. Do NOT permanently convert it to AI.
- **Distinct from DEF-C.** This does not touch and must not regress the connection-handler.ts:59-68
  stale-close guard (reload-beats-close). D15 is the disconnect-lands-mid-await case no existing guard
  reconciles.
- **Claude's Discretion (CONTEXT):** whether the reconciliation is inline in `startGame` or a small
  helper, and whether it keys off `this.connected` / `SeatInfo.connected` — provided the
  disconnected-during-start seat is AI-covered AND reclaimable, proven by the race test.
- Because `playerIsAI`/`playerConfigs` in the start op are computed pre-await (`:529,:536-541`), the
  reconciliation covers the seat for the LOOP DRIVER (`this.aiSeats` → `runAITurns`); the already-built
  start op's per-seat isAI need not be rewritten. Confirm the loop actually proceeds (the bot acts) in
  the test rather than only checking the aiSeats list.
</decisions>
