<objective>
SKILLAUTO-01 (B.1): move the human client-playtest stop from every chunk to exactly the three milestones — (1) core-loop complete, (2) scoring/endgame complete, (3) final-acceptance — plus an always-stop for a genuine rules adjudication / open question. Milestone chunks are identified by an EXPLICIT milestone flag set in SKETCH at sketch-derivation time (not inferred at runtime). Non-milestone chunks keep every internal step (test, audit, self-playtest/sim) unchanged; only the human client-playtest stop moves. A UI-less chunk is never routed to a human playtest.

Purpose: This is the highest-leverage autonomy lever — it removes the per-chunk human interrupt that otherwise halts an autonomous run after every chunk, while keeping the meaningful milestone gates.
Output: SKETCH template milestone flag + sketch-derivation anchor assignment, milestone-gated human-gate list in state-machine.md, milestone/UI-gated playtest dispatch, and fail-pre/pass-post drift tests across three suites.
</objective>
