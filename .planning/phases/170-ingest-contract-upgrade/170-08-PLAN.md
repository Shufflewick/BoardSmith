<objective>
Close INGEST-02 — the requirement that has now failed three consecutive real runs at zero
`Visual (p.` lines — by making the dispatch prompt a filled template and by making each slice carry
a machine-checkable receipt that the transcription contract actually arrived.

Purpose: `170-PROOF-RUN.md` records two facts that must both be respected. **Experiment 1:** a
subagent handed the transcription contract verbatim produced the correct `Derived`/`Visual` split on
the exact page (`seven` p.2) that failed twice in situ. The instruction is correct and effective when
it reaches the subagent. **The failure that remains:** commit `724befd7` already moved that contract
into `ingest/transcription-subagent.md` for the subagent to read directly, and the live run still
produced zero `Visual` lines — so it is the **orchestrator's dispatch step** that is not being
followed. The indirection is right; it is not yet sufficient.

Two structural mechanisms, chosen because both are the kind the failed run proved survive:

1. **The dispatch prompt becomes a template.** `transcription.md` currently describes the dispatch
   prompt in prose around a fenced block. Experiment 2 showed prose specs diverge and template fills
   do not. Moving the prompt into `templates/TRANSCRIPTION-DISPATCH.template.md` and instructing the
   orchestrator to fill three substitutions puts it in the category of artifact that comes out right.
2. **Each slice carries a line-kind receipt.** The subagent must end every slice with a literal block
   naming the contract file it read and counting its own quote, derived, and visual lines. This is
   the diagnostic the last three runs lacked: a missing receipt means the contract never arrived; a
   receipt reporting zero visual lines on a graphically dense page means it arrived and was
   misapplied. Those are different bugs with different fixes, and until now they were
   indistinguishable from the outside. It is also a forcing function — a subagent that must count its
   visual lines cannot fail to notice it has no category for the art note it just wrote.

Corroborating evidence for mechanism 2: runs 1 and 2 both invented a `## Visual notes` heading — a
string appearing nowhere in any skill text — and filed `Derived (p.N):` lines beneath it. The
subagent correctly identified the content as visual and had no prefix available for it. That is the
signature of a contract that did not arrive, and this plan teaches the harness to detect it by name.

**Acceptance bar.** The contract tests added here are fast regression pins that catch a reword
deleting a required string. They are **not** the acceptance bar. Three separate skill-text fixes to
INGEST-02 were each verified installed and each produced zero observable change in live behavior
while their contract tests stayed green. This plan is accepted when `npm run harness:ingest` reports
`visual-lines`, `derived-purity`, `slice-receipt`, and `no-invented-visual-heading` all PASS against
a live agent run.

**What is NOT proven until that run is green.** Nothing in this plan is known to work. Two prior
mechanisms for this exact requirement looked sound on paper and changed nothing.
</objective>
