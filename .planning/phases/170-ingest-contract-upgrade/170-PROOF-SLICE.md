# Solo Variant (p.2)

> Variant (p.2): Solo Variant — the entire section below is a single-player variant. Out of scope by default.

p.2, Solo Variant:
Playing the single-player variant is the same as playing the regular game, the only difference is that you are scoring against yourself.
To start, just try to get a higher total score for each match.
After you've conquered that, attempt to get a multiple of 7 as your final score in each game during the match. As a reminder multiples of 7 are 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, and 49. Therefore for a perfect solo match you would have to achieve a score of 1 of each in no particular order.
And after you've conquered that, attempt to get one of each of the 7 scoring hands in each game during the match in no particular order.
