|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-171-13 | Tampering | hand-edited provenance block | mitigate | Fenced machine-owned with an in-file explanation; a missing fence is a hard, nameable error and the command refuses to write rather than guessing a range |
