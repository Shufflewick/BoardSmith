<objective>
Land PROV-03: a read-only aggregation command that reports verification drift across every chunk, so
`/bs-check-status` formats rather than computes. Three states, never two — full,
code-conformance-only, and **unknown** for a chunk verified before this phase existed.

Purpose: all 29 existing chunks across the two reference games are `unknown` today. That is the
ready-made proof target, and it is precisely the case that must not be reported as
`code-conformance-only` — those chunks were not partially verified, they were verified before
provenance was recorded at all.
Output: `boardsmith chunk-provenance-status [--json]`, plus the `verifiedWithoutProvenance` flag that
makes a skipped `chunk-check` invocation visible.
</objective>
