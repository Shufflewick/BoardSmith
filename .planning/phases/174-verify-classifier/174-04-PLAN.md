Ship the three run-scoped commands that make classification recordable and resumable, and register
them in the real CLI entry point.

so classification inherits crash-safety and resumability instead of re-earning them — one run, one
ledger. Decisions 3, 6, and 8 all push the same way: the subagent supplies one enumerated label plus
evidence, and every consequence of that label is computed here, in code, with a test.

registrations.
