# Expected: `contradictory`

Both sides assert an order of resolution for the same two simultaneous actions, and the orders are
opposite. In any game situation where the two actions would produce different outcomes depending
on order (e.g. Attack removing a Guard that would otherwise block Defense), the two readings
diverge — the two cannot both be true. Reversed precedence over the same actions is `contradictory`,
not `sharper`, because pass 2 does not merely add detail pass 1 lacked; it asserts the opposite of
what pass 1 asserted.
