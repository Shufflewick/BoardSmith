# Expected: `cosmetic`

Pass 1 orders the three setup steps "shuffle, deal, reveal"; pass 2 orders them "shuffle, reveal,
deal". Dealing and revealing the discard pile's top card have no dependency on each other or on
being interleaved with the deal — no game situation produces a different outcome depending on
which of the two happens first. A reordering with no consequence is `cosmetic`, not `sharper`: the
test is equivalence of outcome, not identical sequencing of prose.
