# Visual Identity Survey — 1-2 Punch

Evidence only — no design decision is made here. The visual direction is decided at the FIRST UI
chunk's `ask` step, against `DESIGN.md`. Built exclusively from the `visualEvidence[]` returned by
the transcription pass; the rulebook's visuals are not re-opened downstream, so this file is the
only durable record of them.

## Palette candidates

- Bold boxing-red (roughly `#c8202f`) — the cover field, every section heading, all action-card
  names and icons (p.1, p.2).
- Deep navy blue — the second player color (p.1, p.2).
- Cream / off-white card stock tone; white text on red; body copy dark gray/black on white (p.1, p.2).

Red and navy are the two player colors as well as the document's brand colors — the boxer/player
identity and the brand palette are the same two hues.

## Typography feel

- Heavy condensed all-caps sans-serif for the title and headers (p.1).
- A red script/brush face for section headings — Contents, How to Win, Starting a New Game,
  Starting a New Round, Action Cards, Punch Examples, End of Game, Tips (p.1, p.2).
- Italic serif/sans for flavor text and worked examples (p.1, p.2).
- Bold sans for card names and bolded conditional lead-ins ("If not:", "If you are in your
  corner:") (p.2).

## Iconography

- Star motif flanking the title; a boxing-glove pair icon; a stopwatch icon (p.1).
- One small red glyph per action card: lightning bolt = Jab, chevrons = Retreat/Advance, X = Block,
  glove = Punch, dot = Rest (p.1, p.2).

## Card / board art

- Action cards are depicted with a corner timing number, a large center numeral, a side label
  (e.g. JAB / RETREAT), and a color-coded icon (p.1).
- Guard cards are double-sided boxer-portrait cards with a READY face and an EXHAUSTED face (p.2).
- Bottom-right of p.2 carries a full-color, flat-vector character illustration: a blue-gloved boxer
  landing a hit on a red-gloved opponent (matches the "CHARACTER ART" credit).
- Colophon block with the ALRIGHT GAMES logo and a "not for under 3" warning icon; © 2020 Alright
  Games (p.2).

## Diagrams

- **Setup diagram (p.1)** — three dashed-outline ring areas labeled *blue corner*, *center ring*,
  *red corner*, with down-arrows. Blue boxer in the blue corner, red boxer in the center ring, red
  corner empty. Scattered face-up cards (blue Retreat, red Retreat, blue numbered action cards)
  around it illustrating the opening discards.
- **Plan diagram (p.1)** — blue and red boxer cards at the top, two face-down card backs beneath
  each, with inward arrows labeled *first action* and *second action*.
- **Fight diagram (p.1)** — a red JAB card showing `01` and a blue RETREAT card showing `02` side by
  side, with an arrow labeled *timing* pointing at the corner number.
- **Punch example 1 (p.2)** — three boxer-portrait Guard cards labeled READY / EXHAUSTED /
  EXHAUSTED, arrow to a two-card result labeled READY / EXHAUSTED.
- **Punch example 2 (p.2)** — three Guard cards all labeled READY, arrow to three cards labeled
  READY / READY / EXHAUSTED.
