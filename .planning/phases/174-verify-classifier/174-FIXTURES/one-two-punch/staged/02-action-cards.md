# Action Cards (p.2)

p.2, Action Cards — JAB (TIMING: 1) Quickly strike your opponent!:
If both boxers are in their corners: Do nothing. You can't reach your opponent!
If not: Flip one of your opponent's Guard cards from ready to exhausted. If all their Guard cards are already exhausted, do nothing. A Jab cannot break a Guard card (see Punch).

p.2, Action Cards — RETREAT (TIMING: 2) Backpedal into your corner.:
If you are already in your corner: Do nothing. You're already against the ropes!
If you are in the center ring: Move your boxer to your corner.

p.2, Action Cards — ADVANCE (TIMING: 3) Go on the offensive!:
If you are already in the center ring and/or your opponent also plays an Advance card for the same action: Do nothing. You're just butting heads!
If not: Move your boxer to the center ring. If your opponent is in the center ring, also push them into their corner. For example, Blue is in their corner and Red is in the center ring. Blue moves into the center ring, and Red is pushed back into their own corner.

p.2, Action Cards — BLOCK (TIMING: 4) Cover up to block the incoming punch!:
If your opponent plays a Punch card for the same action: Ignore any effect their Punch has on you (see Punch). Block actions do not block Jabs.
If not: Do nothing.

p.2, Action Cards — PUNCH (TIMING: 5) Hit 'em where it hurts!:
If you are in your corner: Flip one of your Guard cards from ready to exhausted, then resolve your Punch. If you do not have any ready Guard cards, do nothing. You're up against the ropes!
OR
If you are in the center ring: Resolve your Punch without flipping your Guard card. You've got 'em on the ropes!

p.2, Action Cards — PUNCH resolution bullets:
- If your Punch is blocked (see Block) or both you and your opponent are in your respective corners: Your Punch has no effect on your opponent; however, you must still exhaust your own guard card if you are in your corner.
- If your Punch is not blocked and your opponent has at least one exhausted Guard card: Break one of your opponent's exhausted Guard cards by removing it from the game and returning it to the box. You are one step closer to a knockout!
- If your Punch is not blocked and your opponent does not have any exhausted Guard cards: Instead of breaking their Guard, flip one of your opponent's Guard cards from ready to exhausted.
- If both you and your opponent play a Punch for the same action while one player is in the center ring: The player punching from their corner must finish exhausting their Guard card (if able) before either player may resolve their Punch. For example, if Blue is in their corner and Red is in the center and they both Punch, Blue must first exhaust a Guard before both players Punch one another.

p.2, Action Cards — REST (TIMING: 6) Take a breather!:
Reclaim your discards: Take all the action cards in your discard pile and add them to your hand. Do not take any of the action cards currently in play
If any of your Guard cards are EXHAUSTED: Flip one Guard card back to ready. This is in addition to reclaiming your discards.

p.2, Fight phase continuation (printed at the foot of the Action Cards column):
When you are done comparing and resolving the first action cards, DO NOT discard them yet. Leave them in place and flip the second action cards face up. Then, compare and resolve the second action cards just like you did with the first action cards.

Derived (p.2): There are six distinct action-card types with fixed timings — Jab 1, Retreat 2, Advance 3, Block 4, Punch 5, Rest 6.
Derived (p.2): Each player's 8 action cards comprise these six types plus duplicates; the tips confirm each player has two Rest cards.
Derived (p.2): Sequencing — within the Fight phase the first action pair is fully compared and resolved before the second action pair is flipped; cards stay in place until the Discard phase.
Derived (p.2): Jab exhausts one ready opponent Guard but can never break (remove) a Guard; it does nothing when both boxers are in their corners or when all opponent Guards are already exhausted.
Derived (p.2): Legality of reach — attacks only connect when at least one boxer is in the center ring; corner-vs-corner attacks do nothing.
Derived (p.2): Retreat moves the acting boxer from the center ring to their own corner; from the corner it has no effect.
Derived (p.2): Advance moves the acting boxer from their corner to the center ring, and pushes an opponent occupying the center ring back into that opponent's corner. Mutual Advance in the same action slot cancels — both do nothing.
Derived (p.2): Block only negates an opponent's Punch played in the same action slot; it never negates a Jab and otherwise has no effect.
Derived (p.2): Punching from your own corner costs you one of your own ready Guards (exhausted); punching from the center ring is free. With no ready Guard and in your corner, Punch does nothing at all.
Derived (p.2): A Punch that connects breaks (permanently removes) one exhausted opponent Guard if any exists; if the opponent has no exhausted Guard, it instead exhausts one ready Guard.
Derived (p.2): A blocked Punch, or a Punch thrown corner-to-corner, still costs the corner puncher their own Guard exhaustion — the self-cost is paid regardless of effect.
Derived (p.2): In a mutual Punch where one player is in the center ring, the corner puncher's self-exhaustion is fully resolved before either Punch's effect, which can change how many exhausted Guards are available to break.
Derived (p.2): Rest returns all action cards from the acting player's discard pile to hand, excluding cards currently in play, and additionally flips one exhausted Guard back to ready if any Guard is exhausted.
Derived (p.2): Guard state is a three-value track per Guard card — ready, exhausted, or broken/removed — and only Punch removes a card from the game.
