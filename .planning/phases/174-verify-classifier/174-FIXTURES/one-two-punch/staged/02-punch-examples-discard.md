# Punch Examples and 3) Discard (p.2)

> Note: the "3) DISCARD" phase here completes the round structure begun on p.1
> (see `01-round-structure.md`).

p.2, Punch Examples (first example):
If you are punched and have one ready and two exhausted Guards, you would lose one exhausted Guard.

p.2, Punch Examples (second example):
If instead, you have three ready and no exhausted Guards, you would simply exhaust one card, leaving you with one exhausted and two ready.

p.2, 3) DISCARD:
After you have compared and resolved both the first and second sets of action cards, place your two action cards played this round into your discard pile, and then begin a new round. DO NOT DISCARD ANY CARDS UNTIL THIS PHASE.

Derived (p.2): Worked example 1 — Guard state {1 ready, 2 exhausted} hit by a connecting Punch becomes {1 ready, 1 exhausted}, one exhausted Guard removed from the game.
Derived (p.2): Worked example 2 — Guard state {3 ready, 0 exhausted} hit by a connecting Punch becomes {2 ready, 1 exhausted}; nothing is broken.
Derived (p.2): Cards played this round enter the discard pile only in the Discard phase, after both action pairs have resolved — so a Rest played in the same round cannot reclaim that round's own played cards.
Derived (p.2): After the Discard phase, a new round begins immediately; play loops Plan → Fight → Discard until a win condition triggers.

Visual (p.2): First Punch example is a before/after strip of Guard cards — left group of two cards labeled "READY" and "EXHAUSTED", a right-pointing arrow, then a right group of two cards labeled "READY" and "EXHAUSTED". Each Guard card shows an illustrated boxer portrait above a label band naming its state.
Visual (p.2): Second Punch example is a before/after strip — three cards labeled "READY" "READY" "READY", a right-pointing arrow, then "READY" "READY" "EXHAUSTED".
Visual (p.2): Guard card art convention — cream/kraft card with a boxer illustration filling most of the face and a bold all-caps state label ("READY" / "EXHAUSTED") in a band at the bottom; the exhausted face shows the boxer in a more defensive/worn pose.
