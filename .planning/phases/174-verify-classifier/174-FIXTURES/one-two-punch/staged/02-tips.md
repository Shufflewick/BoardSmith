# Tips and colophon (p.2)

p.2, Tips:
- You should never have both rest cards in your hand at the same time. If you do, you likely accidentally picked up your current action cards while Resting. When Resting, only pick up the cards currently in your discard pile and not the two action cards from that round.
- If you are in your corner, you need a ready Guard card for a Punch to work; however, you may still choose to play a Punch card but it won't have any effect. This would not force you to break your own Guard card.
- The state of the game may change in the middle of a round. For example, if your opponent Jabs and exhausts your last ready Guard just as you are attempting to Punch from your corner, you will no longer have a Guard card to exhaust.
- In the plan phase, you may place any action cards, regardless of their timings, in any order. The timing on the cards does not matter in this phase. For example, you can play a Rest (timing 6) as your first action and a Jab (timing 1) as your second action.
- In the fight phase, the first set of action cards are always completely resolved before the second set of action cards. For example, a Block from the first action will not stop a Punch from the second action.
