# Distribution of Cards & Credits (p.1)

p.1, Distribution of Cards:
There are numbers ranging from 1-7 in 4 colors, with 4 copies of each card. In addition, there
are 7 bonus point cards.

Derived (p.1): The numbered deck is 7 numbers x 4 colors x 4 copies = 112 numbered cards.
Derived (p.1): Total deck size is 119 cards (112 numbered + 7 bonus point cards).
Derived (p.1): Each bonus point card is worth +1 point, as printed on its face.

## Worked example (exact contents of the distribution chart)

Row 2 (p.1): green cards 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 — annotated "x 4".

Visual (p.1): The distribution chart is a 4-row grid of card thumbnails, one row per color
(red, green, blue, purple), each row running 1 through 7 left to right with an "x 4" multiplier
label to the right of the row. A fifth short row below shows a single black card face reading
"+1" in white bold with an "x 7" label.
Visual (p.1): Card faces are portrait rounded rectangles (~2:3), solid color fill, large centered
white bold numeral, small white pips ringing the numeral — the pip count appears to track the
card's number.
Visual (p.1): The bonus point card is the only black card face; the four suit colors are a
saturated red, green, blue, and purple against the sheet's golden-yellow ground.
