# Round (Simultaneous) (p.1)

p.1, Round (Simultaneous):
Each player draws 2 cards into their hand. Each player discards 1 card from their hand face-up
off to the side of the table in their own discard pile. If you ever lose track of which round you
are on, count your discards, you will have 7 discards when the game is over. Discards are
considered public information.
If everyone has 10 cards, end the game; otherwise begin the next round.

Derived (p.1): The round has a fixed two-step sequence: draw 2, then discard 1. All players act
simultaneously rather than in turn order.
Derived (p.1): Each round increases a player's hand size by a net +1 card (draw 2, discard 1).
Derived (p.1): Starting from 3 cards, a full game is 7 rounds (3 + 7 = 10 cards), which matches
the 7 discards stated as the end-of-game count.
Derived (p.1): The end-of-game check is made at the end of a round, when every player holds 10
cards; if that is not yet true, another round begins.
Derived (p.1): Discards remain face-up in per-player piles and may be inspected by any player
(public information); a player's discard count is the legal round counter.

Visual (p.1): The heading reads "Round" in bold black with "(Simultaneous)" appended in
lighter/non-bold weight, the only parenthetical qualifier used in any heading on the sheet.
