<objective>
Decide WHICH chunks need repair and WHAT re-opens the playtest gate, write the staleness markers
behind the adjudication gate, and expose the whole thing on the CLI with a `--json` shape Phase 176
consumes.

Purpose: VERIFY-06 (decisions 10, 11, 12, 13) and VERIFY-05's write orchestration (decisions 3, 8,
15, 16, 17). Decision 10 makes `drift-check.ts` the sole authority for code movement — never a second
hash scheme. Decision 11/12 map `clean` → keep `verified` + re-verification stamp and `drifted` →
`built` + gate re-opened. Decision 13 makes a `verified (user-waived)` chunk re-open the gate on code
change rather than auto-re-waiving. Decision 15 forbids capping the stale fraction. Decision 16 hands
Phase 176 the line-level `attributions[]`, not just the stale boolean.

This phase DECIDES the disposition; Phase 176 performs the repair and executes the status flip.

Output: the repair-gate computation, the impact-map status command, the guarded apply command, and
CLI registration.
</objective>
