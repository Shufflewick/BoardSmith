## ⚠️ First: a collision you need to know about

The output directory **already contained slices for my exact page range**, written ~2 minutes before my dispatch (`01-overview-and-setup.md`, `01-starting-a-new-round.md`, `02-action-cards.md`). That prior run was **incomplete** — it stopped after Action Cards and never covered End of Game, Tips, or the colophon.

I did not silently overwrite. I read all three, transcribed independently against the PDF, and kept mine because the prior set has **verbatim errors under citation prefixes** — the one thing a citation prefix promises:

| Prior text | Actual source |
|---|---|
| "…one another in the ring**!**" | "…one another in the ring**.**" |
| "place them in any order face-down **next to** your boxer" | "**in front of** your boxer" |
| "determine the order **of play**" | "determine the order" |
| "pushed back into their corner" | "pushed back into their **own** corner" |
| "while **one player** is in the center ring" | "while **your player** is in the center ring" *(source's own odd wording — preserved)* |
| "exhaust a **Guard card** before **both players** Punch" | "exhaust a **Guard** before **players** Punch" |

The two non-overwritten stale files are moved to `slices/superseded/` — non-destructive, and out of a `slices/*.md` glob. Delete them once you've confirmed.

**Two findings worth surfacing to the designer:**

1. **"Higher timing" is inverted from its plain reading.** The rule says the higher timing resolves first, but the source's own worked example resolves Jab (1) before Retreat (2). Lower numeral = acts first. I flagged this as `Derived` in two slices rather than "correcting" the quote.
2. **Tips is load-bearing, not advice.** Bullets 2-5 are the only statements of: an unpayable Punch is legal-but-inert, costs are evaluated at resolution time (not plan time), timing doesn't constrain placement, and Block is slot-scoped. A downstream chunk that treats Tips as flavor will lose four rules.
