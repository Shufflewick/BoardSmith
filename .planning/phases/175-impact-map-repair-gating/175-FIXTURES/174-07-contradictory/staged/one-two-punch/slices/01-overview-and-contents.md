p.1, Introduction:
1-2 Punch is an easy to learn strategic boxing card game that pits two players against one another in the ring. Predict your opponent's moves to land the perfect punch!

p.1, Contents:
2 Boxer Cards, 16 Action Cards, 6 Guard Cards, 1 Rules Sheet

p.1, How to Win:
Knock out your opponent by breaking all three of their Guards.

Derived (p.1): The game is strictly 2-player.

Derived (p.1): Components divide evenly between the two players by color: 1 Boxer card, 8 Action cards, and 3 Guard cards each (from 2 / 16 / 6 total, with setup step 1 assigning all cards of one color to each player).

Named-but-undefined (p.1): per-player Action Card composition — the rulebook states 16 Action Cards in total and instructs each player to take all cards of their color, but never states how many copies of each action (Jab, Retreat, Advance, Block, Punch, Rest) a player holds. The Tips section on p.2 implies at least two Rest cards per player ("You should never have both rest cards in your hand at the same time"); no other per-card count is given.
