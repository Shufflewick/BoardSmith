# Starting a New Game — Setup (p.1)

p.1, Starting a New Game:
1. Each player must choose a color (red or blue) and take all cards of that color.

p.1, Starting a New Game:
2. Designate three areas on the table to make up the ring. The area directly in front of each player is their respective corner and the area in-between them is the center ring.

p.1, Starting a New Game:
3. The blue player must start with their boxer card in their corner and the red player must start with their boxer card in the center ring.

p.1, Starting a New Game:
4. Each player must do the following:
a. Place your three Guard cards next to you with the ready sides face up.
b. Place one of your Rest cards face up next to you to create your own discard pile.
c. Hold the remaining cards in your hand hidden from your opponent.

p.1, Starting a New Game:
5. If you are the blue player, choose one card from your hand and discard it. Next, you must name a card that the red player must discard. These do not have to be the same cards and are in addition to the Rest cards both players already discarded. Neither of these additional discards may be a Rest card.

p.1, Starting a New Game, worked example:
For example, Blue discards their Retreat and tells Red to discard their Punch.

Derived (p.1): The ring is a three-zone track: blue corner — center ring — red corner. A boxer card occupies exactly one zone at a time.

Derived (p.1): The game starts asymmetrically — Blue's boxer begins in Blue's own corner while Red's boxer begins in the center ring. Only one boxer starts in the center.

Derived (p.1): Setup asymmetry is balanced in the other direction by step 5: Blue pays for the safer starting position by losing two action cards from the pool (one of Blue's own choosing, one dictated to Red), while Red loses only the one card Blue names.

Derived (p.1): Every Guard card starts ready side up, so each player begins with three ready Guards and zero exhausted Guards.

Derived (p.1): Each player's discard pile starts non-empty — it is seeded with one Rest card face up. A player's second Rest card therefore begins in hand.

Derived (p.1): Guard cards, the seeded Rest card, and the discard pile are all public/face-up information; only the remaining action cards in hand are hidden.

Derived (p.1): The step-5 discards are permanent removals from the round-to-round cycle only in the sense that they enter the discard pile — they are recoverable later via Rest (p.2), which reclaims the whole discard pile.
