## Round structure

p.1, Starting a New Round:
Each round has three phases: Plan, Fight, and Discard. Both you and your opponent must complete each phase at the same time.

## 1) Plan

p.1, 1) Plan:
Choose any two action cards from your hand and place them in any order face-down next to your boxer. The card closest to your boxer is your first action and the other card is your second action. Both you and your opponent have the same set of cards and may look through each other's discard pile at any time.

Derived (p.1): The Plan phase commits exactly two action cards per player per round, ordered first then second, and that order is fixed by proximity to the boxer card.

Derived (p.1): Discard piles are open information to both players; hands are hidden (setup step 4c).

Visual (p.1): Plan-phase diagram shows two face-down card backs per player in a vertical pair — blue backs on the left, red backs on the right — with italic labels "first action" and "second action" on the outside of each column and arrows pointing inward toward the cards. Above the pairs sit the two face-up boxer cards (blue-trimmed at left, red-trimmed at right). Card backs carry a repeating small-motif pattern in the player's color.

## 2) Fight

p.1, 2) Fight:
You and your opponent must flip your first action card face up at the same time and compare their timings to determine the order of play.

p.1, 2) Fight:
The player with the higher timing on their card must resolve their action first. If the timing is the same on both cards, they are resolved at the same time.

p.1, 2) Fight (example):
For example, if Red played a Jab and Blue played a Retreat, Red would resolve their Jab first since it has a timing of 1 and Retreat has a timing of 2.

Derived (p.1): "Higher timing" as used here means the lower printed timing number — the worked example resolves timing 1 (Jab) before timing 2 (Retreat).

Derived (p.1): Equal timings resolve simultaneously rather than in any player order.

p.2, (paragraph closing the Action Cards column, bottom-left):
When you are done comparing and resolving the first action cards, DO NOT discard them yet. Leave them in place and flip the second action cards face up. Then, compare and resolve the second action cards just like you did with the first action cards.

Derived (p.2): The Fight phase runs two full compare-and-resolve passes per round — first action cards, then second action cards — with the first pass fully resolved before the second is revealed.

The right card is blue-accented in the same layout: "02" flag and large blue "02" numeral, a blue line-art glyph of angled lines, and "RETREAT" running vertically up the left edge. An arrow at the far right labeled "timing" points at the large numeral, identifying it as the timing value.

## 3) Discard

p.2, 3) Discard:
After you have compared and resolved both the first and second sets of action cards, place your two action cards played this round into your discard pile, and then begin a new round. DO NOT DISCARD ANY CARDS UNTIL THIS PHASE.

Derived (p.2): Played action cards remain on the table through both resolution steps and only enter the discard pile in the Discard phase, so they are unavailable for the rest of the round but visible.
