# About & Setup (p.1)

p.1, About:
Seven is a rummy-style card game for 1 to 7 players that plays in about 7 minutes, and is
recommended for ages 7 and up.

p.1, Setup:
Give each player a "Ways to Score" card. Create a mess of face-down cards in the middle of the
table and mixes them around. Each player should then draw 3 cards from the mess to form their
hand.

Derived (p.1): Player count is 1-7; the 1-player case is handled by the Solo Variant (p.2).
Derived (p.1): Every player begins with a hand of exactly 3 cards, drawn from the shared
face-down "mess" (no shuffled, ordered deck is used — cards are mixed face-down on the table).
Derived (p.1): The "Ways to Score" card is a per-player reference card, one per player, dealt
during setup.

Named-but-undefined (p.1): "Ways to Score" card

Visual (p.1): The entire rules sheet is a single landscape page set on a saturated golden-yellow
background with black body text, laid out in four newspaper-style columns with bold black
section headings. No setup diagram is drawn; setup is described in prose only.
