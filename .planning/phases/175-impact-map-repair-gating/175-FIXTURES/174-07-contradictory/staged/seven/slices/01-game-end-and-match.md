# Game End, Match End, Match Length (p.1)

p.1, Game End:
After all players have 10 cards, the game ends. Each player scores their best hand of 7 cards,
and adds any bonus point cards (even beyond the hand of 7) to their score.

p.1, Match End: Best of 7 Games:
A match consists of 7 games. Play 7 games and keep track of each player's score total. Whichever
player has the highest total score at the end of the match wins!

p.1, Match Length:
A full match of Seven is naturally played in a best of 7 series. That said, if you would like a
shorter or longer match, feel free to play a match as a best of 3 games or a best of 10 games.

Derived (p.1): Scoring uses a best 7-card subset of the 10-card final hand; the player chooses
which 7 cards to score.
Derived (p.1): Bonus point cards score in addition to the 7-card hand and are exempt from the
7-card limit — they count even when held among the 3 cards left outside the scored hand.
Derived (p.1): The match winner is decided by cumulative score across all games, not by games
won.
Derived (p.1): Default match length is 7 games; 3 games and 10 games are offered as alternatives.

> Variant: Shorter/longer match lengths (best of 3 games, best of 10 games) — out of scope by
> default; the default match is 7 games (p.1, Match Length).

Named-but-undefined (p.1): scoring values for hands ("scores their best hand of 7 cards" — the
per-hand point values live on the "Ways to Score" card, which is not reproduced in the rulebook)
