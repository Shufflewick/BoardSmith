# Solo Variant (p.2)

> Variant: Solo Variant (single-player) — out-of-scope by default (p.2).

p.2, Solo Variant:
Playing the single-player variant is the same as playing the regular game, the only difference is
that you are scoring against yourself.
To start, just try to get a higher total score for each match.
After you've conquered that, attempt to get a multiple of 7 as your final score in each game
during the match. As a reminder multiples of 7 are 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, and 49. Therefore for a
perfect solo match you would have to achieve a score of 1 of each in no particular order.
And after you've conquered that, attempt to get one of each of the 7 scoring hands in each game
during the match.

Derived (p.2): The solo variant changes no rules of play — setup, rounds, game end, and scoring
are the regular game; only the win condition is replaced by self-set goals.
Derived (p.2): Solo goals are tiered in stated order: (1) beat your previous match total,
(2) score a multiple of 7 in each game of the match, (3) achieve one of each of the 7 scoring
hands across the match's games.
Derived (p.2): A "perfect solo match" under goal 2 is achieving each of 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, and
49 exactly once across the 7 games of a match, in any order.
Derived (p.2): There are exactly 7 distinct scoring hands.
