### Ruling 202 (CONSTRUCTED — 176-06 lexicon regression, not a real game ruling)

- Decision: A tied bid in the bidding phase is broken in favor of whichever tied player placed
  their bid FIRST (first-bidder-wins).
- Citation interpreted or overridden: n/a — the rulebook is entirely silent on how tied bids are
  broken.
- Rationale: Designer ruling at ingest, needed to resolve simultaneous bids of equal value without
  a re-bid round, which the rulebook never describes.
