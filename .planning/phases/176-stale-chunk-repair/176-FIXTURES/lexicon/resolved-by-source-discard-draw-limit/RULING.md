### Ruling 101 (CONSTRUCTED — 176-06 lexicon regression, not a real game ruling)

- Decision: A player may draw at most ONE card from the discard pile during their own turn.
- Citation interpreted or overridden: n/a — the rulebook is entirely silent on how many cards, if
  any, a player may draw from the discard pile in a single turn.
- Rationale: Designer ruling at ingest, needed to prevent a player from cycling the entire discard
  pile into their hand in one turn, which would break the game's card-scarcity economy.
