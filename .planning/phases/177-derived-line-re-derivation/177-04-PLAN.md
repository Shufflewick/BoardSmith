<objective>
Write the two judgment contracts decision 7 requires: a BLIND derivation contract that never sees the
original, and a SEPARATE comparison contract that does.

Purpose: deriving a value and judging your own agreement with the original in one pass invites
post-hoc rationalization — the model reconciles toward whatever it just saw. Two genuinely separate
contracts, with two distinct handshake tokens and non-overlapping inputs, is what makes the second
opinion a second opinion. A single shared token or a shared context block would risk one contract
serving both roles, which is exactly the anchoring failure the phase exists to prevent.
Output: `derive-recheck.md`, `derive-compare.md`, installer probes, and drift pins.
</objective>
