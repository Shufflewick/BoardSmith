<objective>
Close GAP 2 (CR-01) and its adjacent regex-drift warnings: make the blind-payload strip filter and
the candidate enumerator structurally decoration-proof, so the "independent second opinion"
guarantee is a property of `buildBlindDerivePayload` rather than an accident of the two reference
games' current content.

guarantee 177-CONTEXT.md decision 5 demands ("Independence must be structural: the original line is
not in the dispatch payload at all") is currently defeated by ordinary markdown decoration. This
plan makes it structural.

Output: a shared `annotationBody` helper, a widened shared `DERIVED_LINE_RE`, a payload-level
backstop throw, empirically-proven regression tests, and the WR-09 presentation-marker symmetry fix.
</objective>
