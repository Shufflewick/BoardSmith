p.2 (panel -12-), CARD EFFECT ICONS:
"Damage - Hits player's shields first, if blocked, then HP if any carries over."
"Power / Sentience - Move the corresponding token up or down the Doom Machine track."
"Power + Sentience - Increase or decrease both Power and Sentience."
"Broken Shield - When activated, only 1 shield dice can be placed next turn."
"Destroy Effect - Immediately triggers when a part card is destroyed."
"Repair - Restores part card's HP (only up to its maximum starting health)."
"Gear - Instantly triggers an additional track cycle for any cards immediately adjacent to it."
"Lock - Choose one of your die and set it aside. It does not get rolled or placed on your next turn."
Icon art description (p.2, panel -12-): eight circular icon badges in a column, each ringed in a distinct color on a dark teal card back —
- Damage: a yellow/green starburst or explosion glyph.
- Power / Sentience: two side-by-side badges — a battery/power-cell glyph (Power, black/teal) and an eye glyph (Sentience, yellow).
- Power + Sentience: an eye glyph badge (shown for the combined effect).
- Broken Shield: a green cracked shield glyph.
- Destroy Effect: a yellow circled X.
- Repair: a yellow heart with a triangle. (Corrected from cards.pdf card faces, read at 300 DPI; the earlier "wrench in a purple-pink ring" reading was inaccurate. RULES TEXT unaffected.)
- Gear: a cogwheel glyph in a blue ring.
- Lock: a padlock glyph in an orange ring.
