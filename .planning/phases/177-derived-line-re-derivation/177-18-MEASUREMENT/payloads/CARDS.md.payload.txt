- **18 distinct faces total.**
three squares running DOWN the left edge, then two more running RIGHT along the bottom.
Position 1 is always the **yellow HP die slot** (the die is printed there showing the card's starting HP as pips).
Positions 2–5 hold icons, blanks, or dead spaces. Order of travel = 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5.
Three kinds of space appear:
- **Icon space** — a live space with an effect badge. Connected to its neighbours by a YELLOW connector bar.
- **Blank space** — a live space, plain dark-teal, no icon (no effect fires). Also YELLOW-connected.
- **Dead space** — a DARKENED, diagonally-HATCHED square. Connected by dull grey/teal bars, not yellow.
Per the rulebook (`02-machine-phase.md`), landing on a dead space sends the die back to position 1.
Derived: the yellow-vs-grey connector colour is the reliable tell for live-vs-dead; the hatch fill corroborates it.

1. **Power / Sentience track effect — NOT on the tracker card.** The Doom Machine tracking card is silent
(see p.1 r1c2 above for its complete verbatim contents). The tracks' only printed consequences live on five
machine part cards (Extinction Collider, Synaptic Disruptor, The Doom Core, Cerebral Amplifier, Stasis
Generator). Nothing in either PDF says what happens when a track reaches 10, or whether the tracks can
cause a loss. This remains an unanswered rules question.
2. **"<" operator is undocumented.** STASIS GENERATOR uses `[?] < [3]`. The rulebook's DICE ROLL SYMBOLOGY panel
documents `+`, `=`, `/`, and `>` only. Meaning inferred by symmetry; needs a designer ruling.
3. **Dead-space wraparound gap.** DESIGN FLAW (p.1 r3c3) and EXTINCTION COLLIDER (p.3 r1c1) have **no dead space**
— all 5 cycle-track spaces are live. The rulebook only defines the die returning to the start when it would
land on a dead space, so it does not say what happens when the die is on space 5 of these two cards and must
cycle again. Presumably it wraps to space 1, but that is not printed.
4. **Card count vs. Hard Mode.** There are exactly **15 non-Core machine part cards + 1 Doom Core = 16 cards**.
Hard Mode's "instead of removing 6 cards at random, use all 16" is therefore consistent, PROVIDED the count of
16 includes the Doom Core. Normal setup: 15 parts − 6 removed = 9 parts, stacked on the Doom Core → a 10-card
machine deck, matching `01-objective-and-setup.md` step 3 ("Place the 9 remaining cards face down on top of
the Doom Core"). **No contradiction.**
5. **Repair icon art.** `02-card-effect-icons.md` describes Repair as "a wrench/tool glyph in a purple-pink ring".
On the actual cards (Recycling Vats, Populace Grinder) the Repair icon is a **yellow heart containing a
triangle**. Worth correcting in the icon slice.
6. **Soul Harvester strip text.** `00-visual-survey.md` records "TAKE 1 DMG"; the card actually reads
"LOCK 1 DIE". Correct the survey.
**None.** Every card name, HP die pip count, roll-condition glyph, cycle-track space, effect strip, and destroy
banner in cards.pdf was resolved at 300 DPI. No value was guessed.
