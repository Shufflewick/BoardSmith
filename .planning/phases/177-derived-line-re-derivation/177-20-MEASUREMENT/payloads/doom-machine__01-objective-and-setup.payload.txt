p.1 (panel -1-), OBJECTIVE:
"Uncover and destroy The Doom Core before you succumb to total extinction."
p.1 (panel -1-), SETUP:
"1) Set the Doom Machine tracking card to your left. Place corresponding tracking cubes on the zero spaces of the Sentience (yellow) and Power (black) tracks."
"2) Find the "Doom Core" machine part card, and place it face down to the right of the Doom Machine tracking card."
"3) Shuffle the remaining machine part cards and remove 6 at random (set them back in the tin, as they won't be used for this run). Place the 9 remaining cards face down on top of the Doom Core to create the machine deck."
"4) Place the Player HP (health points) tracking card below the machine deck. Set the white tracking cube on the 10 space of the Player HP track."
p.2 (panel -2-), SETUP (CONTINUED):
"5) Draw the top 3 cards from the machine deck and place them face up, in a line, to the immediate right of the deck. These are the starting machine parts you will face."
"6) Take one yellow die for each revealed machine part card and place it on the yellow dice slot on the cycle track on each card, setting the die face to match the indicated machine part's HP (health points)."
"7) Set the remaining unused yellow dice aside for now, then place your 5 black starting dice in front of you. Setup is complete!"
p.2 (panel -2-), SETUP LAYOUT (diagram legend, verbatim):
