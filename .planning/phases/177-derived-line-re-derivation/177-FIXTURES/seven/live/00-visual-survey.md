# Visual Identity Survey — Seven

Evidence only. No design decision is made here. This file is the durable handoff to the FIRST UI
chunk's `ask` step, where the actual visual direction is decided against `DESIGN.md` (which does not
exist yet, by design). Built exclusively from the transcription subagents' returned
`visualEvidence[]` — the rulebook's visuals are never re-opened downstream.

## Palette candidates

- **Saturated yellow** — the dominant p.1 page background, with black headings and black body text. (p.1)
- **Solid purple with white text** — the entire p.2 background; a strong, deliberate contrast against
  p.1's yellow. (p.2)
- **Card colors: red, green, blue, purple** — flat, saturated. These are load-bearing, not decorative:
  color is one of the two card attributes (with number). (p.1, Distribution of Cards)
- **Black** — the bonus "+1" card, which reads as a deliberate odd-one-out against the four saturated
  card colors. (p.1)

Note the palette is high-contrast, flat, and unshaded throughout — no gradients, textures, or
illustration are reported anywhere in the source.

## Typography

- Bold sans-serif section headings; plain sans-serif body copy. (p.1, p.2)
- p.1 uses a four-column landscape layout. (p.1)
- The title "SEVEN" appears in bold italic white sans-serif, rotated diagonally, on p.2. (p.2)

## Iconography and card art

- **Numbered cards:** flat saturated rounded rectangles in one of four colors, with a large centered
  white numeral and small white corner pips. (p.1)
- **Bonus card:** black, with a white "+1". (p.1)
- Approximate card aspect ratio: **~2:3 portrait**, inferred from the depicted card images. (p.1)
