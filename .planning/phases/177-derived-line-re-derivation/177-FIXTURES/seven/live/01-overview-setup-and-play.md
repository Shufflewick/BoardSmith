# Overview, Setup, Round, and Game/Match End

p.1, About:
"Seven is a rummy-style card game for 1 to 7 players that plays in about 7 minutes, and is recommended for ages 7 and up."

p.1, Setup:
"Give each player a "Ways to Score" card."
"Create a mess of face-down cards in the middle of the table and mixes them around."
"Each player should then draw 3 cards from the mess to form their hand."

p.1, Round (Simultaneous):
"Each player draws 2 cards into their hand."
"Each player discards 1 card from their hand face-up off to the side of the table in their own discard pile."
"If you ever lose track of which round you are on, count your discards, you will have 7 discards when the game is over."
"Discards are considered public information."
"If everyone has 10 cards, end the game; otherwise begin the next round."

p.1, Game End:
"After all players have 10 cards, the game ends."
"Each player scores their best hand of 7 cards, and adds any bonus point cards (even beyond the hand of 7) to their score."

p.1, Match End: Best of 7 Games:
"A match consists of 7 games."
"Play 7 games and keep track of each player's score total."
"Whichever player has the highest total score at the end of the match wins!"

p.1, Match Length:
"A full match of Seven is naturally played in a best of 7 series. That said, if you would like a shorter or longer match, feel free to play a match as a best of 3 games or a best of 10 games."

> Variant (p.1): Match Length — alternate match lengths (best of 3 games, best of 10 games) instead of the default best of 7. Out of scope by default.

Named-but-undefined (p.1): "Ways to Score" card

Named-but-undefined (p.1): bonus point cards (referenced in Game End as adding to score; the +1 card appears in the Distribution of Cards diagram, but no scoring value is defined in the text)

Derived (p.1): The round structure is draw 2 / discard 1, netting +1 card per round; starting at 3 cards and ending at 10 cards means 7 rounds, matching the 7 discards stated as the count at game end.

Derived (p.1): Rounds are played simultaneously by all players, not in turn order.
