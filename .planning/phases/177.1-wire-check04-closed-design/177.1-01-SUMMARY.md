- `derived-line-pattern.ts` now exports `ANNOTATION_FAMILIES` (the ONE list Phase 178 widens), `ANNOTATION_CITATION_RE`, `ANNOTATION_VOCABULARY_RE`, `annotationLineStartRe(family)`, and `ANNOTATION_CITATION_SOURCES` — all built mechanically from `ANNOTATION_FAMILIES`, all zero-import, preserving the leaf-module/no-cycle constraint the file's own header comment documents.

## Decisions Made

- Consolidated regexes are built by `.map().join()` over `ANNOTATION_FAMILIES` rather than as separate hand-copied literals, so a fourth family (Phase 178's `Example`) requires editing exactly one array.
- `ANNOTATION_CITATION_SOURCES` was added to `derived-line-pattern.ts` per the plan's action spec even though no current consumer uses it (the one candidate consumer, `verify-classify.ts`, was measured non-equal — see Findings) — it exists as the correctly-shaped export for a future consumer, not dead code invented ahead of need, since the plan's `<action>` explicitly specified it as this task's deliverable.
- The grep gate's snippet list targets the four specific hand-spelled SHAPES that existed pre-consolidation (joint citation alternation, joint vocabulary alternation, `Visual (p.[^)]*)`  single-family anchor, `Named-but-undefined (p.[^)]*)` single-family anchor) rather than a broad "family name + `(p.`" substring check. A broad check would have false-positived on `verify-classify.ts`'s legitimately different `PRESENTATION_EXCLUSION_MARKERS` array, whose entries share the `Visual (p.` / `Derived (p.` prefix but diverge immediately after (citation body `\d+` not `[^)]*`, plus a qualifier-group suffix the consolidated shapes never have).

## Findings (recorded per plan instruction, not silently resolved)

**`verify-classify.ts`'s `PRESENTATION_EXCLUSION_MARKERS` is NOT element-wise derivable from `ANNOTATION_CITATION_SOURCES`.** Measured directly:

- `ANNOTATION_CITATION_SOURCES` has exactly one bare entry per family (`Derived`, `Visual`, `Named-but-undefined`), citation body `\d+`, no qualifier tolerance: `^Derived \(p\.\d+\):`, `^Visual \(p\.\d+\):`, `^Named-but-undefined \(p\.\d+\):`.
- `PRESENTATION_EXCLUSION_MARKERS` has three entries shaped around presentation SCHEMA VARIANTS, not families: `^Visual \(p\.\d+\)(?: \([^:]*\))?:`, `^Derived \(p\.\d+\) — diagram description(?: \([^:]*\))?:`, `^Derived \(p\.\d+\) — art(?: \([^:]*\))?:`. Two of the three are `Derived`-family (pre-170 legacy qualifiers); `Named-but-undefined` never appears (it is never a presentation note); every entry carries a `(?: \([^:]*\))?` qualifier-group tolerance WR-09 added, which the family-shaped sources never have.

Forcing the derivation would either drop the two `Derived` schema-variant entries (regressing the WR-09/177-08 fix) or invent a `Named-but-undefined` presentation marker that has never existed in real data. Per the plan's explicit instruction, `PRESENTATION_EXCLUSION_MARKERS` was left as its own literal, with a comment at its definition site naming this exact difference. This is the plan's anticipated "Claude's Discretion" outcome, not a deviation from what was asked.
