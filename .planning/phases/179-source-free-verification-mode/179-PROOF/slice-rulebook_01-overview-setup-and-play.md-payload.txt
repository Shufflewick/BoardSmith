3: p.1, About:
4: "Seven is a rummy-style card game for 1 to 7 players that plays in about 7 minutes, and is recommended for ages 7 and up."
6: p.1, Setup:
7: "Give each player a "Ways to Score" card."
8: "Create a mess of face-down cards in the middle of the table and mixes them around."
9: "Each player should then draw 3 cards from the mess to form their hand."
11: p.1, Round (Simultaneous):
12: "Each player draws 2 cards into their hand."
13: "Each player discards 1 card from their hand face-up off to the side of the table in their own discard pile."
14: "If you ever lose track of which round you are on, count your discards, you will have 7 discards when the game is over."
15: "Discards are considered public information."
16: "If everyone has 10 cards, end the game; otherwise begin the next round."
18: p.1, Game End:
19: "After all players have 10 cards, the game ends."
20: "Each player scores their best hand of 7 cards, and adds any bonus point cards (even beyond the hand of 7) to their score."
22: p.1, Match End: Best of 7 Games:
23: "A match consists of 7 games."
24: "Play 7 games and keep track of each player's score total."
25: "Whichever player has the highest total score at the end of the match wins!"
27: p.1, Match Length:
28: "A full match of Seven is naturally played in a best of 7 series. That said, if you would like a shorter or longer match, feel free to play a match as a best of 3 games or a best of 10 games."
