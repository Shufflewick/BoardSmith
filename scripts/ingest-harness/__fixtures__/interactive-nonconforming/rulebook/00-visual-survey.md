## Provenance Caveat (read first)

The transcribed source is the rulebook of a game branded **SEVEN**. This project is scaffolded
as **Proof** per the designer's explicit clean-room re-ingest decision. Everything below is a
faithful record of *the source's* visual identity — it is **not** a recommendation that Proof
adopt it. Whether Proof keeps, adapts, or replaces this identity is decided at the first UI
chunk's design `ask`, not here.

## Palette Candidates

- **p.1 field:** a single saturated **golden-yellow / amber**, full-bleed edge to edge, with a
  thin white outer margin. All type black.
- **p.2 field:** a single saturated **purple** (approx. `#800080`), full-bleed, with a thin
  white page border. All type white.
- **Card palette:** bright **red, green, blue, purple** for number cards; **black** for the
  bonus card. Numerals white and bold, with small white pip dots in the card corners.
- Derived observation: the two pages use *opposing* high-contrast schemes (black-on-yellow,
  white-on-purple) rather than one shared page treatment.

## Typography Feel

- Plain grotesque **sans-serif** throughout (Helvetica / Arial feel). No serif face, no display
  face, no ornament anywhere in the document.
- p.1: bold black headings, regular black body, tight compact leading, four narrow columns.
- p.2: bold small section heading, regular body, tightly leaded, one narrow left-hand column
  occupying roughly a quarter of the page width.
- The **SEVEN logo lockup**: heavy **bold italic condensed** white capitals, rotated ~45°
  (tilted, reading upward left-to-right), upper-right of the p.2 purple panel. The only graphic
  element on that page.

## Iconography

- **Effectively none.** No suits, pips beyond the corner dots, dice faces, card backs, tokens,
  or symbolic icons appear in the document.
- The card thumbnails are the only icons present. No board art, no player-aid illustration.

## Embedded Component Images & Diagrams

Nothing re-reads the source, so these descriptions are the durable record:

- **Set example** (p.1, Definitions) — three card thumbnails in a row: **green 5, green 5,
  purple 5**.
- **Run example** (p.1, Definitions) — three card thumbnails in a row: **red 1, blue 2, red 3**,
  while the caption text reads `example: 5, 6, 7`. *(Discrepancy preserved; see INDEX.md.)*
- **Distribution diagram** (p.1) — a five-row grid of card faces: red 1-7 `x 4`, green 1-7
  `x 4`, blue 1-7 `x 4`, purple 1-7 `x 4`, then a single black `+1` card `x 7`.
- **SEVEN logo lockup** (p.2) — described under Typography above.
- **No setup diagram exists anywhere in the document.** Worth noting: the rulebook describes
  setup ("mess" in the middle of the table, a "Ways to Score" card per player) purely in prose,
  so the first UI chunk has no illustrated table layout to work from.

## Layout Feel

- p.1: dense, four-column, functional — no rules, no boxes, no ornament. A working rules spread.
- p.2: minimal, high-contrast, single-column, heavily asymmetric with large negative space; the
  right three-quarters is intentionally empty. Reads as a back-cover page, not a rules spread.

## Card Proportions

Card thumbnails are drawn as **rounded rectangles, taller than wide, roughly 0.7 w:h**. This
ratio is **depicted in the artwork, never stated as dimensions** in the text — treat it as
approximate. Recorded in `ASSETS.md` on the same caveat.
