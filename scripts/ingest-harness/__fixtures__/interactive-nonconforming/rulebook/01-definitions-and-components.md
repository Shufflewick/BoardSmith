# Definitions, Card Distribution, and Credits (p.1)

p.1, Definitions:
Hand: The cards each player holds. Each starts with 3 and ends the game with 10.
Set: 2+ cards with matching numbers. example: 5, 5, 5
Run: 3+ cards in numeric order. example: 5, 6, 7

Diagram (p.1, Definitions — Set example image): Immediately below the "Set" text sits a row of three card thumbnails, each a rounded rectangle with a large bold white numeral centered on a solid color field and small white pip dots in the corners. Left to right the cards are: green 5, green 5, purple 5.

Diagram (p.1, Definitions — Run example image): Immediately below the "Run" text sits a row of three card thumbnails in the same style. Left to right the cards are: red 1, blue 2, red 3. (Note: the printed Run example text reads "5, 6, 7" while the accompanying image depicts 1, 2, 3.)

p.1, Distribution of Cards:
There are numbers ranging from 1-7 in 4 colors, with 4 copies of each card. In addition, there are 7 bonus point cards.

Diagram (p.1, Distribution of Cards): A grid of card thumbnails, five rows deep. Each of the first four rows shows the numerals 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 left to right on a single solid color, with the annotation "x 4" printed to the right of the row. The four rows top to bottom are: red 1-7 (x 4), green 1-7 (x 4), blue 1-7 (x 4), purple 1-7 (x 4). Beneath them, a fifth row shows a single black card bearing a bold white "+1", annotated "x 7". Every card thumbnail is a rounded rectangle, taller than wide, with a large centered white numeral and small white pip dots arranged in the corners.

Derived (p.1): The depicted number cards total 4 colors x 7 numbers x 4 copies.
Derived (p.1): The black "+1" card shown x 7 corresponds to the "7 bonus point cards" named in the Distribution of Cards text.

Named-but-undefined (p.1): bonus point cards (the "+1" card face is depicted, but no scoring rule for it is given in this section)

Visual notes (p.1): The whole page is one solid saturated golden-yellow (amber) field edge to edge, with a thin white outer margin. All type is black except the card thumbnails. Typography is a plain bold/regular sans-serif (Helvetica/Arial feel), bold headings, tight compact body copy, four-column layout. The card palette is a bright primary-ish set: red, green, blue, purple, plus black for the bonus card, all with white numerals and white corner pips. Iconography is minimal — the card thumbnails themselves are the only icons on the page. No board, no setup diagram other than the distribution grid and the two definition example rows.
