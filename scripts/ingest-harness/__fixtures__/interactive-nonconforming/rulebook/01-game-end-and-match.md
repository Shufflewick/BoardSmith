# Game End and Match Structure (p.1)

p.1, Game End:
After all players have 10 cards, the game ends.
Each player scores their best hand of 7 cards, and adds any bonus point cards (even beyond the hand of 7) to their score.

p.1, Match End: Best of 7 Games:
A match consists of 7 games.
Play 7 games and keep track of each player's score total.
Whichever player has the highest total score at the end of the match wins!

p.1, Match Length:
A full match of Seven is naturally played in a best of 7 series.
That said, if you would like a shorter or longer match, feel free to play a match as a best of 3 games or a best of 10 games.

> Variant: The "Match Length" alternatives — playing a match as a best of 3 games or a best of 10 games — are presented by the rulebook as optional shorter/longer alternatives to the standard best of 7 series. Out of scope by default.

Named-but-undefined (p.1): bonus point cards (scoring value not defined in this section)
Named-but-undefined (p.1): best hand of 7 cards (the scoring method for a hand is not defined on this page; see the "Ways to Score" card / p.2)

Derived (p.1): Scoring happens once per game, after the game-end condition is reached.
Derived (p.1): A player's match score is the running total of their per-game scores across the games of the match.
