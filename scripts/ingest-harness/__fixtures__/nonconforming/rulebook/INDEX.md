## Open Questions (deferred at ingest by the designer)

These are rules the rulebook NAMES but never DEFINES. They were recorded in the slices as
`Named-but-undefined` rather than reconstructed, and the designer deferred them at ingest.
They must be resolved at the `ask` step of the chunk that first needs them.

1. **"Ways to Score" card contents** — every player receives one at setup
   (`01-about-and-setup.md`), and p.2 refers to "the 7 scoring hands"
   (`02-solo-variant.md`), but the 7 hands and their point values are never listed.
2. **"+1" bonus card value** — the card face reads `+1` and the rules say bonus point cards
   "count even beyond the hand of 7" (`01-game-end-and-match.md`), but the scoring value and
   whether multiples stack are never stated.
3. **"Best hand of 7" selection** — at game end a player holds 10 cards and scores their best 7
   (`01-game-end-and-match.md`), but whether that is a single 7-card combination, a partition
   into multiple sets/runs, or something else — and whether it is computed automatically or
   chosen by the player — is never stated.
